EMX Soul Arena v12 — Story World Update

Install:
1. Open this zip.
2. Open the emx_soul_arena_v12 folder.
3. Copy everything inside that folder into your GitHub repository root.
4. Your repo root should contain index.html, style.css, script.js, manifest.webmanifest, multiplayer.html, multiplayer.css, multiplayer.js, firebase-config.js, firebase-rules.json, and assets/.
5. Commit and push from SPCK.
6. Wait for Vercel to redeploy.
7. Open the Vercel link in Safari and test.

Do not upload the folder as emx_soul_arena_v12/index.html unless your Vercel root directory is set to that folder.

New v12 features:
- EMX City Hub clickable world map
- Story Chapters with boss dialogue and tips
- Interactive Choice Rooms between battles
- Neon Tower challenge mode
- Pet Lab: feed, train, play, choose companion
- Better Boss Phases and warning counters
- Power Loadouts, max 6 equipped powers
- Sticker Book 2.0 for enemies, bosses, zones, classes, pets, powers
- Weekly Events with claimable rewards
- Player Profile Card with name, badges, stats, favorite class

Testing checklist:
1. Open the main menu and tap Open Full EMX City.
2. Read a Story Chapter and mark it read.
3. Open Neon Tower and clear a floor.
4. Open Pet Lab and feed/train/play with the pet.
5. Open Power Loadouts and save a loadout.
6. Start a run and make sure powers outside the loadout are locked.
7. Fight a boss and look for phase warnings.
8. Open Sticker Book 2.0 and check unlocked stickers.
9. Open Weekly Events and claim the weekly reward.
10. Open Profile Card and save a player name.
